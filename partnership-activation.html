How it works

A structured process, built around your expansion goals.

Step 01
ICP & Expansion Brief

A focused intake session to capture your product positioning, target customer profile, geographic priorities, and the ecosystems you operate in. This brief drives every downstream decision.

Step 02
AI-Scored Partner Matching

We run your brief against our database — scoring partners across capability, market fit, geographic reach, ecosystem activity, and customer overlap — and surface the top candidates.

Step 03
Outreach & Qualification

On the Discovery tier we hand you the shortlist. On Activation, we engage the partners directly — handling first contact, qualification conversations, and objection responses on your behalf.

Step 04
Signed Letters of Intent

We convert engaged conversations into signed commitment documents — giving you tangible evidence of pipeline and a foundation for the commercial agreements that follow.

Common questions

Answers to the questions we hear most.

"We can do this ourselves with LinkedIn."
LinkedIn shows you who exists. We tell you who is likely to activate — based on partner activity patterns, customer overlap, ecosystem engagement, and commercial model fit. The difference is TurboTax versus a blank spreadsheet. And we run the outreach, which is the part that actually takes time.
"We already use WorkSpan or Tackle."
Those tools manage relationships you've already established. We solve the problem that comes before that — identifying which relationships to build in the first place. We're upstream of every co-sell execution tool on the market, not a replacement for them.
"ChatGPT can do this research for $20 a month."
Generic AI gives generic answers. Our matching is built on ecosystem-specific data: SAP partner tiers, Microsoft competencies, marketplace listing activity, and 20+ years of VAD relationship intelligence. That specificity is what produces a shortlist you can actually act on — not one you have to validate from scratch.
"We're not sure channel is the right move yet."
The Discovery tier is a low-commitment way to find out. You get a research-backed shortlist with context on each partner's fit, business model, and readiness. Many clients use it to validate the channel thesis before committing to a full activation programme.
"How do you guarantee 3 LOIs?"
We scope every engagement before we start — and we only accept engagements where we're confident in the deliverable. If we reach the engagement period without hitting the LOI threshold, we extend our active outreach at no additional cost until we do. We'd rather be honest about fit upfront than make a promise we can't keep.
"What ecosystems do you cover?"
We have deep coverage in SAP and Microsoft ecosystems, with strong HubSpot and AWS channel data. Our partner database spans VAR, MSP, SI, and distributor profiles across North America, UK, Benelux, DACH, and the Nordics. If your ecosystem isn't listed, ask us — our data extends further than what we lead with.
